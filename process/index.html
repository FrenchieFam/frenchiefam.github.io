Websites & practical prototypes

No surprises. Here's exactly how it goes.

We start with what you need, agree a small scope and price, then build and review it together. Timing and access are agreed before work starts.

01 / Process

The four steps

  1. 01

    You send the brief

    For a website, send the current link and what you want to change, or describe the new page you need. For automation, tell me the task and which tools you use. Please keep passwords and customer information out of the first message.

    Deliverable: A written scope after we have the details we need.

  2. 02

    I quote and you approve

    We agree what is included, a price, a realistic delivery date and a revision allowance. If the request changes, we agree the extra work before proceeding.

    Deliverable: A one-page scope doc: what's included, what's not, delivery date.

  3. 03

    Build or edit

    I prepare a draft or small demonstration, then check it against the agreed brief. You review the wording and business details before anything goes live.

    Deliverable: Progress, not silence. You always know the state.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    You receive the agreed editable files and setup notes. Hosting, accounts, third-party costs and any ongoing support are agreed separately. Existing third-party licences still apply.

    Deliverable: The agreed files, checks and handoff notes.

02 / Your brief

What I need from you

For a website

  • Current site or example link
  • Any brand assets
  • Services, contact details and approved photos
  • Deadline

For automation work

  • The task description
  • Any existing scripts or workflows
  • Access method: local machine, VPS, or sandbox
  • Success criteria

03 / Boundaries

What I don't do

  • I don't take on projects where the scope isn't clear. I'll ask questions first.
  • Credentials stay in the appropriate account or secret store. Access and data handling must be agreed before any connected work.
  • A prototype is labelled as a prototype. No search rankings, sales or customer results are guaranteed.
