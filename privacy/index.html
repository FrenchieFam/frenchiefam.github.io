Privacy / Last updated November 16, 2026

Your visit and your enquiries.

This notice covers Bryan’s FrenchieFam portfolio and its browser-only demonstrations at frenchiefam.dev. Contact: frenchiefamdigital@gmail.com.

Browsing the portfolio

The portfolio does not include advertising pixels, a visitor analytics script, an account system or a newsletter form. Its demonstrations use fictional information in your browser. They do not send a message, connect to your phone or submit their inputs to Bryan. Please use fictional details when trying them.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting requests can involve technical information such as an IP address and request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. “No form data collected” does not mean that internet hosting creates no technical records.

If you email Bryan

An email enquiry includes the contact details and information you choose to send. Bryan uses it to respond and discuss the requested work. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, or private customer records in an initial enquiry.

For questions about information you have sent, including a request to correct or delete it, email the contact above. Any request needs to be reviewed alongside records that may need to be retained for an agreed project or legal obligation. This notice does not promise a specific retention period or deletion deadline.

Separate forms and services

The separate handyman service-request page asks for a name, phone number, service and ZIP code and sends submissions to Formspree. Formspree stores an archive and forwards requests to the handyman enquiry mailbox. Who can access those records and how long they are retained are being reviewed. This portfolio notice is not a completed privacy notice for that form; please contact Bryan directly if you have questions before submitting details there.

Links to GitHub, Fiverr, Gumroad and other websites take you to separate services with their own privacy practices. Their accounts, checkout and data handling are not controlled by this static portfolio.

Tracking preferences and changes

No advertising or cross-site tracking feature is implemented in the portfolio code, so it does not change its behavior in response to a Do Not Track setting. If forms, analytics or other data-handling features are added here, this notice needs to be reviewed and updated. The date above identifies this version.
